Privacy Policy

Cooper does not collect or share any personal information.

Your countdown (its name, date, color, emoji, and display style) is stored only on your device, where Cooper’s widgets can read it. Cooper never sends it to the developer or anyone else. Like other app data, it may be included in your device’s backups.

Your countdown stays on your device until you delete it. To delete it, open Cooper, tap Edit, then Start Over, or delete the app.

Cooper does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or any third-party services.
