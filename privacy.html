Privacy Policy

Cooper does not collect or share any personal information.

Your countdowns (each name, date, color, emoji, and display style) are stored only on your device, where Cooper’s widgets can read them. Cooper never sends them to the developer or anyone else. Like other app data, they may be included in your device’s backups.

Your countdowns stay on your device until you delete them. To delete one, open Cooper, go to that countdown, tap Edit, then Remove Countdown, or delete the app.

Cooper does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or any third-party services.
